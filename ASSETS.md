# Blog artwork

The homepage uses the cat sitting on a crocodile requested by the owner, from the **Test Automation** section of [Jessie’s blog](https://blog.jessie-ouo.link/).

Original: [x_cat_v1 PNG](https://blog.jessie-ouo.link/assets/images/x_cat_v1-0edc03ce720d700168c4bb36ec3fe5e6.png), 1000 × 1000, retrieved 2026-10-20.

The local 480px, 640px and 960px WebP variants retain the original artwork and transparency, with lossless WebP encoding after resizing. Their filenames include the encoded file’s content hash. The original transparent canvas is retained; CSS positions its visible content within the sky-and-meadow background. There is no image caption, as requested.

The variants are served from this site under `/assets/`, with responsive image selection and immutable caching. The page does not hotlink the image or depend on the blog being available at render time. `public/social.png` is regenerated from the composed homepage and is not loaded as a homepage asset.
